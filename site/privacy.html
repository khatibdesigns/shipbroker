ShipBroker Privacy Policy

How we handle your data.

Last updated: 2 June 2026

Overview

ShipBroker (“we”, “us”) is a two-sided shipping marketplace for the GCC. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices. We do not sell your data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Information we collect

  • Account & profile: name, phone number, email address, postal address, and optionally gender and date of birth that you enter when you sign up.
  • Shipments & requests: pickup and destination you type or search, item details, weight, dimensions, photos you attach, and messages you send to the in-app AI assistant.
  • Identifiers: an account identifier created by our sign-in provider (including anonymous “guest” sessions).

We do not access your device’s GPS location. Place search for pickup/destination is based on text you type.

How we use it

  • To create and manage your shipments and show matching offers.
  • To let the AI assistant identify your item and build a shipment summary.
  • To operate your account, escrow, and delivery tracking.

All processing is to provide the app’s functionality. We do not use your data for tracking or advertising.

Service providers

We use trusted processors to run the service:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication & Cloud Firestore) — stores your account and shipment data securely.
  • Google Maps Platform (Places & Static Maps) — receives the place text you search and renders the route map.
  • Our AI backend — when you use the AI assistant, your message and any photo you attach are sent to our server to generate the shipment summary. Photos are processed transiently and deleted after analysis.

Data retention & deletion

Your profile and shipments are retained while your account is active. You can sign out at any time. To request deletion of your account and associated data, email nader@khatibdesigns.com and we will action it.

Security

Data is stored in your account and protected by security rules that restrict access to you. Payments are held in escrow and released on delivery.

Children

ShipBroker is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected here with a new date.

Contact
