Frequently asked

How do I create a shipment?

On Home, tap “Send with AI” to chat (or attach a photo of your item), or use Account → Create Shipment for the step-by-step wizard. You’ll then see offers to choose from.

How does payment work?

When you select an offer, payment is held securely in escrow and released to the carrier once your delivery is confirmed.

Who can carry my package?

Verified travellers heading your way and licensed logistics companies. Each carrier shows ratings, reviews, on-time performance, and delivery history.

Can I use the app in Arabic?

Yes — tap the English | عربي toggle on any screen for full Arabic with right-to-left layout.

How do I delete my account?

Email nader@khatibdesigns.com from your account email and we’ll delete your account and data.
